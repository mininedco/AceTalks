# Supporting AceTalks

AceTalks is free to build, open to maintain, and built on the belief that communication is a right, not something anyone should have to pay a subscription fee to access basic functions.

Every dollar contributed to AceTalks goes directly toward keeping that promise.

\---

## Where your support goes

**Text-to-speech and translation infrastructure**
AceTalks uses Azure Neural TTS to generate natural-sounding voices in 40+ languages. Every time someone taps a tile and hears a word spoken in Thai, or Haitian Creole, or Vietnamese, that costs a fraction of a cent in cloud compute. Multiply that across thousands of daily users and it becomes the project's largest operating cost. Your support keeps those voices running.

**Database and cloud services**
Boards sync in real-time between a child's tablet and their therapist's device. That requires a database, file storage for symbols, and a backend server — all running 24/7. These are the invisible costs that keep the app working reliably.

**Server infrastructure**

* Azure Cognitive Services — neural TTS voices across all supported languages
* Supabase — database and real-time board sync
* Cloudflare R2 — audio caching so voices respond instantly
* Railway — backend API server
* Vercel — web app hosting

\---

## The Lifetime Access Fund 

This is the part that matters most to us.

The families who need AceTalks most are often the ones least able to afford it. A parent navigating autism diagnoses, therapy appointments, and school IEPs is already stretched. We don't want price to be the reason a child doesn't have a voice.

A portion of every sponsorship goes into the **Lifetime Access Fund** — a pool that sponsors lifetime subscriptions for low-income families who apply. If you or someone you know needs support accessing the full app, reach out. No one is turned away because of cost.

## How to sponsor

**GitHub Sponsors** — one-time or monthly, directly through GitHub. Every amount helps.

**Other ways to support**

* Contribute translations in your language — this saves professional translation costs
* Share AceTalks with an SLP, a special education teacher, or a family who needs it
* Star the repo — it helps other families find the project
