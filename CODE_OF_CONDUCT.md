# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

AceTalks is built for everyone. 

We are committed to making participation in this community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, disability, neurodivergence, communication style, language, level of technical experience, gender identity and expression, ethnicity, nationality, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

This code of conduct applies in all community spaces — GitHub Issues, Pull Requests, Discussions, and any other space where this community meets.

\---

## Our standards

**We expect:**

* Patience with people who communicate differently, type slowly, use AAC themselves, or are not fluent in English
* Welcoming language toward caregivers, clinicians, AAC users, and people who have never written code
* Genuine respect for the lived experience of disability and neurodivergence
* Constructive feedback given with the recipient's growth in mind
* Acknowledging that cultural context shapes communication — a feature that works for one community may not work for another
* Graceful acceptance of corrections, especially on matters of accessibility and lived experience
* Prioritizing the needs of the people who will use this app to communicate

**We do not accept:**

* Harassment, intimidation, or sustained disruption of discussions
* Dismissing accessibility concerns as edge cases or "nice to haves"
* Mockery of communication differences, AAC use, or assistive technology
* Condescending or infantilizing language toward people with disabilities
* Assuming that technical contributors' opinions outweigh the perspectives of AAC users and caregivers
* Publishing others' private information without consent
* Sexualized language or imagery in any project space
* Personal or political attacks
* Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional or clinical setting

\---

## Special considerations for this community

This project serves non-verbal and minimally verbal people, many of them children. We ask that all participants keep this in mind:

* **Children's privacy is sacred here.** Do not share identifying information about AAC users, even with good intentions.
* **Lived experience is expertise.** An AAC user's feedback about the app carries as much weight as a developer's code review.
* **Not everyone communicates the same way.** Short messages, unconventional grammar, or slow responses are not reasons to dismiss a contribution. Meet people where they are.
* **Caregivers are often exhausted.** Parents and support workers contributing here may be doing so between appointments and long days. Extend them patience.

\---

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainer at:

**mininedco@gmail.com** 

All reports will be reviewed promptly and handled with discretion. The maintainer is obligated to respect the privacy and safety of the person reporting.

**Enforcement steps:**

1. **Correction** — a private message explaining what was inappropriate and why, with a request to change the behavior
2. **Warning** — a formal warning with specific consequences for continued behavior
3. **Temporary suspension** — temporary removal from community spaces
4. **Permanent ban** — permanent removal for severe violations or repeated behavior after warnings

\---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces and also applies when an individual is representing the project in public spaces — for example, when posting about AceTalks on social media, speaking at a conference, or representing the project in any official capacity.
